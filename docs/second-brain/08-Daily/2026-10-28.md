# 2026-10-28

## App-only Growth and Scale pricing

- Owner confirmed Growth at ₱499/month with 500 active garments and 5 Front Desk seats, and Scale at ₱999/month with 1,000 garments and 8 seats.
- Both plans inherit Standard's paid features and the same 14-day trial.
- Show Growth and Scale in `/onboarding/plan` and `/settings/billing`; keep the public marketing snapshot at Free, Starter, and Standard only.
- Keep the public/documented Standard garment cap at 300. The temporary database-only Standard quota remains separate from public pricing copy.
