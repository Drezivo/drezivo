to keep a session secure, remember a preference, prevent abuse, measure service reliability, or,
only where approved, support analytics or marketing. This notice supplements the

Where consent is required, Drezivo will present a clear choice before activating the relevant

cookies, though essential parts of the service may no longer work.

The privacy owner must review this notice before enabling a new browser SDK, analytics tool,
advertising pixel, session-replay tool, or third-party script. The release record must identify the
provider, purpose, categories of information, storage period, transfer location, and consent
