## 1. What this notice covers

This notice describes how Drezivo processes personal information through its website, staff
dashboard, public storefront tools, reservation features, support channels, and related services.
It applies to visitors, business account users, staff, guests, and people whose information a
business submits to Drezivo.

When a clothing-rental business uses Drezivo to manage its own customers or guests, that business
usually decides why the information is collected and how long it is needed. In that situation the
business is the personal information controller and Drezivo acts as its personal information
processor under documented instructions. The business must provide its own notice and lawful
instructions. Drezivo may also be a controller for its own account, billing, security, support,
legal, and product-improvement purposes.

## 2. Information we may process

The exact fields depend on the feature and the business configuration. They may include:

- name, email address, telephone number, business role, and account identifiers;
- business name, branch, address, tax or billing details, and service-plan information;
- reservation dates, garment selections, measurements, fitting notes, pickup and return details,
  customer messages, and status history;
- payment status, receipt or payment-evidence metadata, refund details, and limited transaction
  references. Drezivo does not need a full card number to provide the described workflow;
- uploaded images or documents and the technical metadata needed to secure and retrieve them;
- device, browser, IP address, request identifiers, timestamps, security events, and diagnostic
  data; and
- preferences, support correspondence, survey responses, and consent records.

Users must not submit sensitive personal information unless the feature and lawful purpose require
it, the data subject has received the required notice, and the controller has approved the handling.
Drezivo does not ask for health, biometric, government-ID, or financial-account information for a
feature that does not need it.

## 3. Sources

We receive information directly from a person, from the business that operates a storefront, from
identity and payment providers, from devices and browsers, and from service providers acting on
our instructions. A business may also import information under its own responsibility.

## 4. Purposes and lawful bases

We process information only for a specified, legitimate purpose and in a way that is transparent,
proportionate, accurate, and limited to what is needed. Depending on the relationship, the lawful
basis may be:

- performance of a contract or steps requested before a contract;
- compliance with a legal obligation;
- consent, where consent is the appropriate basis and can be withdrawn;
- protection of vital interests; or
- legitimate interests, balanced against the data subject’s rights and freedoms.

Examples include authenticating users, providing reservations and storefronts, sending service
messages, securing accounts, preventing fraud and abuse, supporting customers, billing, keeping
business records, improving reliability, and responding to lawful requests. We do not use personal
information for a new incompatible purpose without the required notice or legal basis.

## 5. Marketing and cookies

Service and administrative messages may be necessary to operate an account. Promotional messages
will be sent only where permitted, with a clear opt-out method. We use only the cookies and similar
technologies needed for login, security, preferences, measurement, or a separately disclosed
purpose. Browser settings can restrict cookies, but some features may stop working.

The final published notice must list the actual analytics, advertising, cookie, and consent tools
enabled in production. A tool must not be named here until it has been approved in the processing
inventory.

The [Cookie Notice](Drezivo-Cookie-Notice.md) explains how Drezivo will disclose the cookies and
similar technologies actually used in production. It must be updated before a non-essential
analytics, advertising, or preference technology is enabled.

## 6. Disclosure and processors

We may disclose information to the business that controls a reservation, staff members authorized
by that business, identity provider Clerk, database provider Supabase, object storage provider Amazon
S3, email and messaging providers, security and monitoring providers, payment providers selected
for a feature, professional advisers, and public authorities when legally required. We do not sell
personal information.

We select processors, document instructions and safeguards, restrict access, and require
confidentiality. A business controller remains responsible for deciding which customer data it
submits and for answering requests that concern its own customer relationship.

When Drezivo processes personal information for a business as its processor, the parties' roles,
instructions, security commitments, subprocessor process, and deletion/return obligations are
described in the [Data Processing Addendum](Drezivo-Data-Processing-Addendum.md).

## 7. International processing

Some providers may process information outside the Philippines. Before production launch, Drezivo
must maintain a current processor and transfer register, assess the destination and safeguards,
include required contractual terms, and state the actual countries or regions where law requires.
This notice should be updated when a material transfer or processor changes.

## 8. Retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the stated purpose, the account relationship, legal
obligations, dispute resolution, security records, or the establishment or defence of legal claims.
The final retention schedule must specify periods for account records, reservations, financial and
tax records, payment evidence, support tickets, security logs, backups, and marketing preferences.
When retention ends, information is securely deleted, anonymized, or irreversibly de-identified.
The internal [Data Retention and Deletion Standard](Drezivo-Data-Retention-and-Deletion-Standard.md)
requires an approved, documented schedule before production processing begins.

## 9. Security

We use reasonable and appropriate organizational, physical, and technical safeguards matched to
the nature and risk of processing. Controls include least-privilege access, tenant and branch
authorization, encryption in transit, protected storage, audit records, validation at boundaries,
secure development, backups, monitoring, incident response, and vendor controls. No internet
service can promise absolute security.

If a personal data breach is likely to create a real risk to data-subject rights and freedoms,
Drezivo and the relevant controller will follow the Data Privacy Act, its implementing rules, and
National Privacy Commission guidance. Where required, notification to the NPC and affected data
subjects is made within seventy-two (72) hours after knowledge of, or reasonable belief that, a
qualifying breach occurred.

## 10. Data-subject rights

Subject to lawful limits, a data subject may ask to be informed, access personal information and
processing details, correct inaccurate information, object to or restrict processing, request
blocking or deletion, request portability where applicable, withdraw consent where consent is the
basis, and lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

Requests about a reservation or customer record should first go to the business named on the
storefront because it may be the controller. Requests about a Drezivo account, website processing,
or this notice may be sent to **[INSERT PRIVACY EMAIL]**. We may verify identity, coordinate with a
controller, and explain a lawful reason if a request cannot be completed.

## 11. Automated processing

Drezivo should not make a decision with significant effect on a person solely through automated
processing unless the lawful basis, notice, safeguards, human review, and any NPC notification or
registration duties have been assessed. The final production inventory must identify any profiling,
recommendation, fraud, or eligibility logic.

## 12. Children

Drezivo is intended for businesses and their customers, not for children to create independent
accounts. A business must not submit a child’s information unless it has a lawful basis, appropriate
parental or guardian handling, and a clear purpose. Contact the privacy address if information was
submitted improperly.

## 13. Changes

We may update this notice when processing, law, providers, or product features change. We will
publish the new version with a new date and provide additional notice when required. Material
changes must be reviewed against the processing inventory and the relevant business controller
agreements.
