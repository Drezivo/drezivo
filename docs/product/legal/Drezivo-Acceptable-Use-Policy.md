# Drezivo Acceptable Use Policy

**Last updated:** [INSERT DATE]
**Contact:** [INSERT LEGAL CONTACT EMAIL]

> with the actual enforcement process, support capacity, and applicable law before publication.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy protects Drezivo, its rental-business customers, and their guests from unlawful,
unsafe, deceptive, or disruptive use. It applies to every person who accesses the staff dashboard,
public storefront, API, upload flow, support channel, or any related service.

Each rental business is responsible for its own catalogue, availability, customer-facing policies,
payment instructions, rental operations, and the conduct of its invited staff. Drezivo is software,
not the merchant for a rental made through a tenant storefront.

## 2. You must not

You must not use Drezivo to:

- break the law, infringe intellectual-property rights, deceive a consumer, harass a person, or
  violate another person's privacy;
- create false availability, reservations, payment evidence, refunds, customer records,
  identities, or financial entries;
- upload malware, harmful code, unlawful content, or material that you do not have the right to
  use, display, or process;
- submit government identification, financial-account data, biometric data, or other sensitive
  personal information unless the feature, lawful purpose, notice, and authorized access are in
  place;
- use another person's account, share credentials to evade a role or branch restriction, bypass
  multi-factor authentication, or attempt to obtain another tenant's data;
- probe, scan, overload, scrape at a harmful scale, reverse engineer beyond what mandatory law
  permits, interfere with the service, or bypass rate limits, access controls, file checks, or
  security controls;
- use automated means to create bookings, harvest customer information, or test payment flows
  without Drezivo's written authorization;
- use the service to operate a marketplace, payment-facilitation service, or a business category
  that Drezivo has not approved; or
- direct Drezivo to process personal information without a lawful basis, required notice, or
  authority to give that instruction.

## 3. Accurate rental and payment information

A rental business must present accurate garment, price, deposit, availability, pickup, delivery,
cancellation, refund, and contact information. A receipt upload is evidence for the business to
review, not proof that funds have settled. The business remains responsible for merchant decisions,
taxes, consumer obligations, and handling rental payments and refunds.

## 4. Account and security responsibilities

Account owners must keep business details current, use individual staff accounts, assign the least
access needed, and promptly remove staff who no longer need access. Owners must report suspected
account compromise, a leaked guest link, unauthorized export, or security incident through
**[INSERT SECURITY CONTACT EMAIL]** without delay.

## 5. Enforcement

Drezivo may investigate a suspected violation, preserve necessary evidence, rate-limit a request,
remove harmful content, require account recovery, restrict a feature, suspend access, or terminate
an account when permitted by the Terms and law. Where practical, Drezivo will give notice and a
chance to correct the issue. It may act without notice when delay could create a security, fraud,
privacy, legal, or safety risk.

Restriction will be proportionate. It does not authorize Drezivo to expose customer data or to
silently erase records needed for an approved return, refund, settlement, export, dispute, or legal
retention obligation.
