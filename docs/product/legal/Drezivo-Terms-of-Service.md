## 1. Agreement and scope

These Terms govern access to Drezivo, a hosted software service for clothing-rental businesses.
The service can include a staff dashboard, public storefront tools, reservation workflows,
inventory records, payment-evidence workflows, reports, and related documentation.

By creating an account, accepting an order form, or using the service, the person accepting these
Terms confirms that they can enter a binding agreement for themselves or the business they name.
If they accept for a business, they represent that they have authority to bind that business.

## 2. Important role of the rental business

Drezivo provides software. Each business using Drezivo remains the merchant and service provider
for its own customers. That business sets rental prices, deposits, availability, fitting policies,
pickup and return rules, cancellation terms, refunds, taxes, customer communications, and the
condition of each garment. A storefront, booking confirmation, or payment instruction is issued
on behalf of that business, not by Drezivo as the rental provider.

Guests should direct questions about a specific garment, booking, charge, deposit, refund, pickup,
return, or complaint to the business named on the storefront. Nothing in these Terms removes rights
that consumers cannot waive under Philippine law.
