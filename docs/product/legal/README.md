# Drezivo Legal and Trust Documentation

> [!warning] Counsel and launch approval required
> These are structured drafts, not legal advice, final contracts, or evidence of security or
> regulatory compliance. Do not publish any document with a placeholder or a statement that has
> not been verified against the production service.

- [Terms of Service](Drezivo-Terms-of-Service.md): agreement with a rental business using Drezivo.
- [Privacy Policy](Drezivo-Privacy-Policy.md): Drezivo's personal-information notice.
- [Acceptable Use Policy](Drezivo-Acceptable-Use-Policy.md): prohibited conduct and enforcement.
- [Data Processing Addendum](Drezivo-Data-Processing-Addendum.md): controller-processor terms for

- [Vulnerability Disclosure Policy](Drezivo-Vulnerability-Disclosure-Policy.md): a safe route for

## Required completion before publishing

1. Insert the legal entity, registered address, DPO, legal/privacy/security contact addresses,
   effective dates, support route, governing venue, billing terms, and the approved export window.
2. Complete a data inventory, Privacy Impact Assessment, processor and international-transfer
   register, cookie inventory, and approved retention schedule.
3. Have Philippine counsel and the appointed Data Protection Officer review the documents and the
   actual data flows. Obtain operational-owner sign-off on every control described publicly.
4. Verify that public copy exactly matches the released product. V1 does not provide an integrated
   payment gateway, automatic payment verification, or universal merchant refund policy.
