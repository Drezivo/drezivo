# Drezivo Data Processing Addendum

**Last updated:** [INSERT DATE]
**Processor:** [INSERT LEGAL ENTITY NAME]
**Privacy contact:** [INSERT PRIVACY EMAIL]

> [!warning] Draft for Philippine legal review
> This addendum is a framework for the business-to-business relationship. It does not replace a
> signed agreement, a Privacy Impact Assessment, or the rental business's own privacy notice. A
> Philippine lawyer and the appointed Data Protection Officer must finalize it before it is offered
> to customers.

## 1. Parties and roles

This Data Processing Addendum (DPA) forms part of the [Terms of Service](Drezivo-Terms-of-Service.md)
between Drezivo and the rental business using the service (Business).

For personal information that the Business submits or collects through its Drezivo storefront for
its own rental operations, the Business is generally the personal information controller and
Drezivo acts as its personal information processor. Drezivo may act as an independent controller
for its own account administration, billing, security, support, legal, and service-improvement
purposes, as described in the [Privacy Policy](Drezivo-Privacy-Policy.md).

## 2. Processing instructions

Drezivo will process Business personal information only to provide, secure, maintain, support, and
improve the service; comply with documented Business instructions that are consistent with the
Terms, this DPA, and applicable law; or meet a legal obligation. Drezivo will notify the Business
when an instruction appears unlawful, unless law prevents notification.

The Business confirms that it has a lawful basis, required notices, and authority for its
instructions and for all personal information it provides. It must not require Drezivo to collect
sensitive data, including government ID, unless a documented purpose, access restriction,
retention period, and approved workflow exist.

## 3. Processing details

| Item | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Subject matter | Hosted rental-business operations, storefront, reservation, customer support, security, and authorized exports. |
| Duration | During the service term and afterwards only for the approved return/deletion period, backups, security, disputes, or legal obligations. |
| Purpose | Run and secure the Business workspace and public storefront, process bookings and evidence, support users, and meet legal obligations. |
| Data subjects | Business owners and staff, guests and customers, people whose records the Business imports, and support contacts. |
| Data categories | Contact and account details; booking, garment, pickup/return, payment-evidence metadata, support, security, and audit information. |
| Sensitive data | Not collected by default. Any approved ID or other sensitive data needs a specific documented workflow and least-privilege access. |

## 4. Confidentiality and security

Drezivo will limit access to authorized personnel and processors who are bound by confidentiality
obligations. It will maintain safeguards appropriate to the risk, including access control, tenant
and branch authorization, encryption in transit, protected storage, logging/redaction, backup and
recovery planning, incident response, and secure development practices.

This clause is an obligation to apply appropriate safeguards, not a promise that a system is
invulnerable or a claim of certification. The customer-facing explanation is in the
[Security Overview](Drezivo-Security-Overview.md); the implementation baseline remains internal.

## 5. Personnel and subprocessors

Drezivo may engage subprocessors needed to operate the service, such as identity, database,
storage, email, monitoring, and support providers. Before production processing, Drezivo must keep
a current processor and transfer register, impose written privacy and security obligations on each
subprocessor, and provide the Business with the agreed notice and objection process for material
subprocessor changes.

The published Privacy Policy must name only processors and locations that are actually in use and
approved. The DPA cannot be treated as approval for an undeclared provider or transfer.

## 6. Assistance and data-subject requests

Drezivo will provide reasonable assistance, considering the nature of the processing and available
information, for the Business to respond to valid requests about access, correction, objection,
blocking, deletion, portability, or consent withdrawal. If Drezivo receives a request that
primarily concerns the Business's customer relationship, it will direct the person to the Business
where appropriate and legally permitted.

The Business remains responsible for deciding the request, verifying the requester's identity,
meeting applicable response deadlines, and telling Drezivo what lawful action to take.

## 7. Personal data incidents

Drezivo will notify the Business without undue delay after confirming a personal-data incident
affecting Business information, unless law prevents it. The notice will contain the information
reasonably available to help the Business assess its obligations. The Business, as controller,
remains responsible for determining and making any required regulator or data-subject notification,
unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.

Neither party may conceal an incident, destroy relevant evidence, or make public statements about
the other party's incident without coordination, except where law requires disclosure.

## 8. International processing

Where a provider processes information outside the Philippines, Drezivo will assess the transfer,
document the safeguards and legal basis required for the transfer, and keep the relevant register
current. The Business will provide reasonable assistance where its controller role requires it.

## 9. Return, deletion, and audit

At the end of the service, Drezivo will make Business content available for export for the approved
export window and then delete or return it as instructed, except where retention is required for
backups, security, legal obligations, disputes, or defence of claims. Retention and deletion must
follow the approved schedule in the
[Data Retention and Deletion Standard](Drezivo-Data-Retention-and-Deletion-Standard.md).

On reasonable written request, Drezivo will provide information reasonably necessary to show its
compliance with this DPA. Any audit must be scoped, confidential, non-disruptive, protect other
tenants, and follow the agreed process. The parties may agree that independent assurance or
documented security evidence satisfies an audit request where lawful and appropriate.

## 10. Priority and governing law

If this DPA conflicts with the Terms on processing Business personal information, this DPA controls
to that extent. Philippine law and the finalized Terms govern this DPA, subject to mandatory law.
