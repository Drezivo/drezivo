## Durable conclusions

- [[02-Architecture/Drezivo Architecture]] remains the system map.
- [[02-Architecture/Tenancy, Onboarding, Clerk, Memberships, and Billing Foundation]] records the
  accepted staged design for owner onboarding, Clerk, memberships, entitlements, and manual billing.

  final active Owner, and requires bounded, rate-limited, generic-failure handling for onboarding,
  invitations, claims, and webhook intake.

  an additive migration and backfill plan, and a loopback-only PostgreSQL/MinIO compose stack for
  local migration rehearsal.
