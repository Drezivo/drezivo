# Drezivo security foundation template

The detailed `SECURITY-FOUNDATION.md` is intentionally kept outside Git. Obtain that private
document through the approved team channel before making security-sensitive changes. Never place
secrets, credentials, customer data, threat intelligence, or incident details in this template.

Until the private guide is available, follow the root `AGENTS.md` non-negotiable rules and stop
before changing authentication, authorization, tenant isolation, storage, logging, deployment, or
incident-response behavior. The private guide must remain local and ignored by Git.
